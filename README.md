Privacy Policy - Mountain Trucks
Last updated: 10 October 2026

Mountain Trucks ("the app") is developed by PRNT. This policy explains how the app handles information.

Information we collect
The app does not collect, store, transmit or share any personal information. It does not require an account, does not show ads and does not use analytics or tracking services.

Data stored on your device
Game progress (unlocked levels, stars, best times) and settings (sound volume, steering mode, graphics quality) are saved only on your device. This data never leaves your device and is removed when you uninstall the app.

Device sensors
If you choose tilt steering, the app reads the device accelerometer only while you play, to steer the truck. This data is not stored or sent anywhere.

Children
The app does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes
If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page.
